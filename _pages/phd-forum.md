The {{ site.conference.acronym }} PhD Forum provides an opportunity for graduate students to present their research in a supportive environment with feedback from experienced researchers.

## Mentorship Program

IDA 2027 will include a mentorship program. Students participating in the PhD track will be paired with experienced researchers (e.g., IDA Senior Program Committee members) and meetings will be arranged during the conference to allow discussion between the students and mentors.

## Submissions

### Who Should Submit

The PhD Forum is open to all graduate students working in {{ site.conference.topic }} or related fields.

It is worth noting that you do not need to have previously published your work. Our PhD Forum is open to all PhD students, including those in the early stages of their research and still developing their research ideas.

### Submission Format

- **2-page paper** (including author names and affiliations)
- **Electronic PDF** format submitted through the submission system
- Select the **"PhD Forum"** track (not the Regular Paper Track)

### What to Include

Your 2-page paper should cover:

1. The problem and why it is important
2. The goal and research questions
3. The planned approach and methods
4. Outline of existing knowledge
5. Expected results

### Poster Requirements

- **Format:** A0 portrait (841 x 1189 mm)
- **Printed by participant**
- Optional: videos or demos
- Tables available for demo/video during the forum
